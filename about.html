THE BARDHAMAN EV ZONE STORY

Built on Community Trust, Engineering Integrity, and Fair Pricing

Located conveniently at City Hotel More, Khagragorh on G.T. Road, BARDHAMAN EV ZONE was established to provide riders in Bardhaman and neighboring towns with certified electric scooters that deliver real-world range without marketing exaggerations.

Unlike unbranded street importers, we represent certified manufacturers whose vehicles undergo rigorous road safety inspections, AIS-156 battery compliance checks, and thermal runaway prevention audits.

Whether you are traveling between Bardhaman University, Curzon Gate, the railway station, or commuting across the district to Memari or Galsi, our electric vehicles ensure a smooth, silent, and virtually zero-maintenance ride.

ENGINEERING PILLARS

The Three Benchmarks of Every EV Zone Vehicle

We rigorously evaluate every component before placing any model on our showroom floor.

Smart Thermal BMS

Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) and high-density NMC battery chemistries monitored by intelligent 16-channel microcontrollers to prevent overcharging, thermal surge, and cell imbalance.

  • ✓ 2000+ Full Charge Cycles
  • ✓ IP67 Dust & Water Immersion Proof

High-Torque Hub Motors

Brushless Direct Current (BLDC) motors engineered with neodymium rare-earth magnets delivering instant torque for effortless flyover climbs and heavy double-riding across rough terrains.

  • ✓ 92% Electrical Efficiency
  • ✓ Zero Belts, Chains, or Oil Changes

In-House Service Center

Our dedicated workshop at City Hotel More stocks authentic OEM controller boards, throttle sensors, brake pads, LED assemblies, and tire replacements so you are never stranded waiting for parts.

  • ✓ Same-Day Routine Maintenance
  • ✓ On-Road Mobile Emergency Support
